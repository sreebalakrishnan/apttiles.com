The Balaghat advantage

It starts with the earth.

APT's manufacturing story is rooted in clay from Balaghat. The family describes it as having strong binding ability, colour and strength—qualities that help the tiles dry gradually and resist cracking even away from India's traditional coastal tile clusters.

How we make

From clay to final inspection.

Seven stages turn Balaghat clay into finished architectural terracotta — prepared, shaped, rested, fired and checked by hand before dispatch.

01EarthClay sourced seasonally
02PrepareGround, processed & de-aired
03ShapePressed into dies
04Rest8–12 days in drying sheds
05DryGradual open-air drying
06FireKiln fired at ~600–1,000°C
07InspectChecked by hand & packed
